# OctoAcme Project Management Process Docs

This folder contains the project management guidance used by OctoAcme to help teams plan, execute, communicate, and improve work across the full lifecycle of a project. The goal is to provide a repeatable operating model with clear roles, visible progress, and a shared definition of how work is prioritized and delivered. These documents are meant to reduce onboarding friction, align teams around common expectations, and make project health easier to track.

OctoAcme’s approach begins with initiation, where a new idea or proposal is validated with stakeholders, business context, and measurable goals. During this phase, the team documents the problem, defines success metrics, and creates a lightweight project one-pager or charter to confirm the effort is worthwhile and worth planning. Once approved, the work moves into planning, where backlog items are prioritized, milestones are mapped, dependencies are identified, and the team defines scope, estimates, and a Definition of Done. Execution follows with delivery in small, testable increments, daily standups, project boards, and regular reviews to ensure the work is progressing as expected.

The process also relies on clearly defined personas and responsibilities. Product leadership sets outcomes and prioritization, project managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communication, developers implement and validate the solution, and QA ensures the work meets acceptance criteria and quality standards. Stakeholders provide input and approvals as needed, while cross-functional teams work together to reduce uncertainty and improve decision-making. Communication is intentionally structured through weekly syncs, standups, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths that provide a way to resolve blockers and raise issues when necessary.

Quality and continuous improvement are built into each stage of the lifecycle. OctoAcme expects acceptance criteria for work, automated testing, security scanning, integration checks, and smoke tests for critical flows before release. Release and deployment guidance promotes safe rollout practices, smoke testing, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication after deployment. Finally, retrospectives capture lessons learned, create action items, and turn improvements into recurring team practices. Together, these processes help OctoAcme maintain a transparent, disciplined, and customer-focused approach to project execution.

## Quick reference by project phase

- Initiation: Define the problem, identify stakeholders, and confirm measurable success.
- Planning: Prioritize backlog work, estimate effort, and align milestones and dependencies.
- Execution: Deliver in smaller increments, run regular standups, and track progress through the project board.
- Release: Validate quality, deploy safely, and communicate changes and rollback plans.
- Retrospective: Capture lessons learned and assign improvement actions with owners and follow-up dates.

## Summary of OctoAcme project management processes

- Initiation: Validate the business need and create a project one-pager with goals, stakeholders, and success metrics.
- Planning: Build a prioritized backlog, estimate work, confirm milestones, and define the Definition of Done.
- Execution & Tracking: Use project boards, small PRs, CI validation, daily standups, and demos to track and deliver work.
- Release & Deployment: Follow pre-release checks, smoke tests, deployment review, and rollback plans for safe launches.
- Risk Management & Communication: Maintain a risk register, communicate status clearly, and escalate issues through the proper channels.
- Retrospectives & Continuous Improvement: Review outcomes, document action items, and apply improvements to future work.
